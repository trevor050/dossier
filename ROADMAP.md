# Roadmap

Things I'd like to do next, roughly in order. None of these are promised.

- **Publish to npm** as `@trevor050/dossier`, with release notes per version.
- **Notifications**: an email or webhook the first time a named link is opened. This is the feature I actually want most.
- **Link expiry and notes** shown on the link page, plus per-link "stop tracking after N days".
- **Replay quality**: jump from a timeline event to that moment in the replay accurately, skip idle by default, and lighter canvas recording for canvas-heavy sites.
- **Datacenter/VPN detection** from enrichment data, to down-weight IP edges from known VPN exits.
- **CSV export** of a person's visits, and a full data export for subject-access requests.

Things it will not do: cross-site tracking, "supercookies" that survive clearing site data, or tricks to evade blockers.
